Methods available in this static site

The directory intentionally contains procedures that can be calculated reliably in the browser without a server-side statistical environment.

Sign Test (G)

Tests whether positive or negative changes predominate, ignoring the size of each change.
One group, pre–postOrdinal / interval

Wilcoxon Signed-Rank Test

Tests a paired shift while taking both direction and magnitude of change into account.
One group, pre–postOrdinal / interval

Paired t-test

Compares the mean pre–post difference. The differences should be approximately normally distributed.
One group, pre–postInterval

McNemar Exact Test

Tests whether the proportion of a binary outcome changes between two paired measurements.
One group, pre–postBinary nominal

Normalized Gain (Hake g)

Describes the proportion of the available improvement that was achieved. It is an effect indicator, not a significance test.
One group, pre–postScores expressed as %

Bespalko Mastery Coefficient (Ka)

Describes mastery as the proportion of essential operations completed correctly; Ka ≥ 0.70 is treated as a mastery threshold in the supplied methodological framework.
Criterion-referenced outcomeProportion

Friedman Test

Tests whether three or more related measurements differ. A significant result requires follow-up paired comparisons.
One group, 3+ measurementsOrdinal / interval

Mann–Whitney U Test

Compares the distributions/ranks of two independent groups without assuming normality.
Two independent groupsOrdinal / interval

Independent Samples t-test (Welch)

Compares group means. Welch’s version is used by default because it does not require equal variances.
Two independent groupsInterval

Fisher’s Exact Test (2×2)

Tests association/difference between two binary proportions using an exact 2×2 calculation.
Two independent groupsBinary nominal

Pearson Chi-Square Test

Tests whether two categorical variables are associated or whether categorical distributions differ.
Independent categorical dataNominal / grouped ordinal

Spearman Rank Correlation

Measures a monotonic association without requiring normality or linearity.
Relationship between indicatorsOrdinal / interval

Pearson Correlation

Measures linear association between two quantitative variables. It is sensitive to outliers.
Relationship between indicatorsInterval

Cronbach’s Alpha

Estimates internal consistency of a multi-item test or scale. It should be checked before the main study.
Measurement qualityItem scores

Cohen’s Kappa

Measures agreement between two raters beyond chance agreement.
Measurement qualityNominal ratings

Kendall’s W

Measures concordance among three or more experts who rank the same objects.
Measurement qualityRanks

One-Way ANOVA

Tests whether at least one group mean differs across three or more independent groups.
Three or more independent groupsInterval
Educational decision-support tool for pedagogical research. Calculators are designed for teaching and preliminary analysis. For thesis publication or high-stakes reporting, verify assumptions and reproduce final analyses in a validated statistical package.
