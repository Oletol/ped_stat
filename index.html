Interpreting evidence

A difference is not automatically an effect of the method.
A statistical test can show a change, difference or association. Attribution to teaching also depends on the study design.

For a stronger conclusion

Use a comparison group where possible, measure the baseline, document group formation and attrition, and use reliable outcome measures.

Report at least

Sample size, descriptive statistics, test statistic, p-value, confidence interval when available, effect size, assumptions and limitations.
