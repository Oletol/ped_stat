Evidence guide

Why your answers matter

Start with the research purpose

The method must answer the question your study actually asks. A test chosen only from the data format may support the wrong conclusion.

What you can conclude

A statistical test can show a change, difference, association, or level of agreement. Whether teaching caused the result depends on the study design.

What you cannot conclude
p < .05 alone does not prove that a course or method was effective.

Report at minimum

  • Method and why it fits the design
  • Usable sample size and missing data
  • Descriptive statistics and test result
  • Effect size and confidence interval
  • Plain-language conclusion and limitations
