Method note

When to use it

ProportionIndividual / groupMinimum: Any n

Describes mastery as the proportion of essential operations completed correctly; Ka ≥ 0.70 is treated as a mastery threshold in the supplied methodological framework.

Conditions and limits:
The threshold must be specified before inspecting the outcome, and the definition of an essential operation must be justified.

How to interpret “effective”

Treat the calculator output as evidence about a statistical pattern. A statistically significant result is not automatically proof that a teaching method is effective. Causal interpretation depends on the research design, baseline comparability, attrition, measurement reliability and alternative explanations.

Educational decision-support tool for pedagogical research. Calculators are designed for teaching and preliminary analysis. For thesis publication or high-stakes reporting, verify assumptions and reproduce final analyses in a validated statistical package.
