Method note

When to use it

Nominal / grouped ordinalIndependentMinimum: Expected counts should generally be ≥5

Tests whether two categorical variables are associated or whether categorical distributions differ.

Conditions and limits:
Expected frequencies should generally be at least 5. Use Fisher’s exact test for small 2×2 tables.

How to interpret “effective”

Treat the calculator output as evidence about a statistical pattern. A statistically significant result is not automatically proof that a teaching method is effective. Causal interpretation depends on the research design, baseline comparability, attrition, measurement reliability and alternative explanations.
