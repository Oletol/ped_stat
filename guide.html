Planning before analysis

What your design allows you to conclude

A calculator cannot repair a weak research design. Use this page to separate evidence of change from evidence that the change was caused by a teaching method.

DesignWhat it can supportMain limitation
Final measurement onlyWhether a predefined standard or mastery threshold is reached.Cannot show that the method produced the observed level.
One group, pre–postWhether outcomes changed during the course and how large the change was.Cannot rule out time, maturation, other courses, motivation, or repeated-testing effects.
One group, 3–4 measurementsWhether a consistent trajectory develops across the course.Still lacks a concurrent counterfactual comparison.
Experimental + control groups, pre–postStronger evidence that change is associated with the intervention, especially with baseline equivalence or randomization.Quality depends on group formation, attrition, measurement validity, and implementation fidelity.

Minimum planning checks

Use equivalent pre/post instruments, record data by participant code, define the alpha level and one- vs two-sided hypothesis in advance, plan for attrition, and avoid selecting the test after inspecting the result.

Small samples

With very small groups, exact/nonparametric methods, effect sizes, confidence intervals and individual trajectories become more important. Failure to reach p < .05 may simply reflect insufficient power.

Language for the Results chapter

AvoidPrefer
“The method was proven effective because p < .05.”“The experimental group showed a statistically significant improvement; this result is consistent with the effectiveness hypothesis, subject to the limitations of the study design.”
“There was no effect because p > .05.”“The analysis did not provide sufficient evidence to reject the null hypothesis at α = .05; the estimate and confidence interval should be considered together with sample size and statistical power.”
“The correlation proves that X causes Y.”“X and Y were statistically associated. The correlational analysis does not establish causal direction.”
